# Coverage and open shifts

Coverage answers one question for administrators: *is someone rostered for every hour that needs support?* The portal highlights the hours where nobody is, and the shifts that nobody has picked up yet.

Coverage is shown to administrators only.

## Set the hours that need cover

1. Open **Admin**, and go to the **Organisation** section.
2. Find **Required coverage**.
3. Under **Coverage**, choose one of:
    - **Set hours**, then choose the **From** and **Until** times, for example 07:00 to 22:00. If the end is earlier than the start, the window runs overnight and the page shows *(ends the next day)*.
    - **24 hours, every day**, for round-the-clock support.
    - **Off (don't highlight gaps)**, if you do not want gaps highlighted.
4. Choose **Save**.

The page shows what is currently set, for example *Current: 07:00–22:00*. Times are in your organisation's time zone. Only an organisation administrator can change this setting.

## What you see on the Week view

Above the week grid, one line sums up the week. For example:

- *No gaps this week*,
- *1 gap · 2h uncovered this week*,
- *No gaps · 1 open shift this week*.

The line has a warning symbol and wording as well as colour, so it does not rely on colour alone.

Inside the grid, each stretch of required time that nobody covers appears as a card that starts with **Uncovered**, followed by the times, for example *Uncovered 07:00–09:00*. Past days show their gaps in the same way, as a record of what happened.

### What counts as cover

- A scheduled shift with at least one worker on it covers its hours.
- An **open shift** (no workers) does not cover anything yet. It is counted, so you can see how many still need someone.
- Cancelled shifts never cover.
- Appointments never cover.

Switching **Show cancelled shifts and appointments** on or off does not change coverage.

## Open shifts

An open shift is a shift with nobody assigned. To fill it, open the shift, tick the worker or workers, and choose **Save changes**. The open shift count and any gaps update when the week reloads.

## When coverage is not checked

Repeating shifts are only planned a limited time ahead. For weeks beyond that, the Week view says *Repeating shifts are only planned 12 months ahead, so coverage isn't checked for this week.*

To plan the shifts that cover each day, see [Plan the roster](planning.md) and [Repeating shifts and edit scopes](templates.md).
