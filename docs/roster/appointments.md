# Appointments

An appointment is something planned for the participant that is not a support shift: a clinic visit, a therapy session, a family event. Appointments sit on the roster beside shifts so everyone can see what is happening.

Administrators add and change appointments. Everyone with access to the roster can see them.

## Add an appointment

1. Open **Roster**, on the **Week** tab.
2. Choose **Add appointment**.
3. Enter a **Title**, for example *Physio*.
4. Choose the **Date**, then the **Start** and **End** times.
5. Add a **Location** if there is one.
6. Choose the **Attending worker**, or leave it as **No one** if no worker needs to attend.
7. Under **Repeats**, choose **Does not repeat**, **Every week** or **Every 2 weeks**. For a repeating appointment you can also set a **Last day**. Leave it empty to repeat until you end it.
8. Add an **Admin note** if you want one. Only administrators and the attending worker can see it.
9. Choose **Add appointment**.

Appointments are planned about 12 weeks ahead and topped up as time goes on.

## How appointments look

On the Week view and on **My shifts**, an appointment carries an **Appointment** badge, its title, time and location, and either *Attending:* and the worker's name, or *No worker attending*. The attending worker sees *You're attending* and the admin note.

An appointment does not count towards [coverage](coverage.md), and it does not send a shift email.

## Change or cancel an appointment

Select the appointment to open it, make your changes, and choose **Save changes**. As with shifts, once an appointment has started its date and times are locked, but you can still edit the title, location, attending worker and note.

To cancel one, choose **Cancel appointment**. It stays on the roster marked **Cancelled**; nothing is deleted. Choose **Reinstate appointment** to bring it back.

Open the **History** tab to see what changed and when.

## Repeating appointments

A repeating appointment is changed the same way as a repeating shift. When you save a change, you choose between **This appointment** and **This and all following**. You see a preview of what will change before you confirm, and appointments you edited individually, cancelled appointments and appointments that have started stay as they are. See [Repeating shifts and edit scopes](templates.md) for the full explanation.

- To change the whole series, find it on the **Templates** tab, choose **Edit**, and use **Apply changes from** to say where the change starts.
- To stop a series, choose **End series** and pick the **Last day**. Later appointments are cancelled and stay on the roster marked as cancelled.
- A series keeps its weekday. To move it to another weekday, end the series and add a new appointment.
