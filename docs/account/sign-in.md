# Sign in

Go to [carersportal.me](https://carersportal.me) and choose **Sign in**.

You can sign in two ways:

=== "Email and password"

    Use the email address your administrator invited. The first time, choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen to set one — you will get an email with a link.

    !!! note "Verify your email first"
        If your email address has not been verified, sign-in is refused with *"Please verify your email address before signing in."* Check your inbox (and junk folder) for the verification email and click the link, then try again.

=== "Google"

    Choose **Continue with Google** and pick the Google account that matches the email address your administrator invited.

## Which email do I use?

The one your administrator added you with. Access is tied to the email address, so `jo@example.com` and `jo.smith@example.com` are different people to the portal. If you are not sure, ask your administrator — see [Access is pending?](access-pending.md).

## Staying signed in

The portal keeps you signed in on that device until you choose **Sign out** from the menu, or until you have been away for a long time. On a shared device, always sign out.
