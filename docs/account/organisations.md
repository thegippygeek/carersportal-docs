# Work in more than one organisation

If you support more than one person — or work for more than one care team — you can belong to several organisations in the portal with **one sign-in**. Each organisation's records stay completely separate; you choose which one you are working in, and you can switch at any time.

You join an extra organisation the usual way: its administrator [invites](../admin/invite.md) the email address you already sign in with. You do not need a second account.

## Choosing an organisation when you sign in

If you belong to more than one organisation, the portal asks **Choose an organisation** after you sign in. Each organisation is listed with your role in it — you might be an *Admin* in one and a *User* in another.

Choose the one you want to work in. Nothing from any organisation is shown until you do.

!!! tip "Skip the question next time"
    Tick **Make this my default — don't ask again when I sign in** before choosing, and the portal will open that organisation straight away from then on. You can change this later — see [Set a default organisation](#set-a-default-organisation).

If you belong to only one organisation, you are never asked — you go straight in, exactly as before.

## Switching organisation

The name of the organisation you are working in is always shown under the Carers Portal logo.

To change it:

1. At the top of the screen, find **Organisation**.
2. Pick the organisation you want from the list.
3. Choose **Switch**.

The portal reloads everything for the new organisation and takes you to its home screen. What you can see and do follows your role **in that organisation**.

!!! note "Finish what you are doing first"
    Switching closes whatever you had open. Save a shift note or record before you switch — anything unsaved is lost.

The **Organisation** switcher only appears if you belong to more than one organisation. It appears once the home screen has finished loading.

## Set a default organisation

1. Choose **Profile**, then the **Preferences** tab.
2. Under **Organisations**, open **Default organisation**.
3. Pick an organisation — or **Ask me each time** to be asked whenever you sign in.

Your default applies on **every device** you sign in on. If you later leave that organisation, or your access to it is removed, the portal simply goes back to asking.

## Several tabs or devices

Each browser tab remembers its own organisation, so you can keep one organisation open in one tab and another in a second tab. Look at the name under the logo to check which one you are in before you add a record.

- **Refreshing** a tab keeps the organisation you were in.
- **Opening a new tab** opens your default organisation, or asks if you have not set one.
- **Signing out** forgets your choices on that device; the next time you sign in you get your default, or the question again.

## Something not right?

- **An organisation is missing from the list.** Your access to it may not have been set up, or may have been removed. Ask that organisation's administrator — see [Access is pending?](access-pending.md).
- **You expected to go straight in but were asked.** Your default may no longer be available to you. Set a new one in **Profile → Preferences**.
- Anything else: [report a problem](../support/report-a-problem.md). Never include a participant's details.
