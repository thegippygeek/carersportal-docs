# Try the demo

The **Try the demo** button on the home page opens a complete, working portal for a made-up participant called Alex, with a small support team and about a month of realistic records.

- No account or password needed.
- You can add, edit and delete records — everything you do stays in the demo.
- The demo resets itself every night, so it is always tidy in the morning.
- You arrive as **Sarah**, a support worker. Use the persona switcher in the header to see the portal as an administrator or as a read-only viewer.

!!! info "What the demo will not let you do"
    A few administrative actions (changing passwords, revoking access, creating display tokens) are locked in the demo so nobody can break it for the next person. The portal explains this where it applies.

Ready for the real thing? Ask the administrator of your care team to invite you — see [Sign in](sign-in.md).
