# Support more than one person

If you support more than one participant — for example as a family member, a support worker or a coordinator — you can see each person's records with **one sign-in**. Each participant's records stay completely separate; you choose whose records you are working in, and you can switch at any time.

You are added the usual way: the administrator of that participant's records [invites](../admin/invite.md) the email address you already sign in with. You do not need a second account.

## Choosing who you are supporting when you sign in

If you support more than one person, the portal asks **Who are you supporting today?** after you sign in. Each person is listed with your role for them — you might be an *Admin* for one participant and a *User* for another.

Choose the person whose records you want to work in. Nothing from anyone's records is shown until you do.

!!! tip "Skip the question next time"
    Tick **Always open this person's records when I sign in** before choosing, and the portal will open their records straight away from then on. You can change this later — see [Choose whose records open first](#choose-whose-records-open-first).

If you support only one person, you are never asked — you go straight in, exactly as before.

## Switching to someone else

The name of the person whose records you are in is always shown under the Carers Portal logo.

To switch:

1. At the top of the screen, find **Supporting**.
2. Pick the person from the list.
3. Choose **Switch**.

The portal reloads everything for that person and takes you to their home screen. What you can see and do follows your role **for that person**.

!!! note "Finish what you are doing first"
    Switching closes whatever you had open. Save a shift note or record before you switch — anything unsaved is lost.

**Supporting** only appears if you support more than one person. It appears once the home screen has finished loading.

## Choose whose records open first

1. Choose **Profile**, then the **Preferences** tab.
2. Under **People you support**, open **Open when I sign in**.
3. Pick a person — or **Ask me each time** to be asked whenever you sign in.

This applies on **every device** you sign in on. If your access to that person's records is later removed, the portal simply goes back to asking.

## Several tabs or devices

Each browser tab remembers whose records it is in, so you can keep one person's records open in one tab and another person's in a second tab. Check the name under the logo before you add a record.

- **Refreshing** a tab keeps you with the same person.
- **Opening a new tab** opens the person you chose under *Open when I sign in*, or asks if you have not chosen.
- **Signing out** forgets your choices on that device; the next time you sign in you get your chosen person, or the question again.

## Something not right?

- **Someone is missing from the list.** Your access to their records may not have been set up, or may have been removed. Ask the administrator for that person's records — see [Access is pending?](access-pending.md).
- **You expected to go straight in but were asked.** The person you chose may no longer be available to you. Choose again in **Profile → Preferences**.
- Anything else: [report a problem](../support/report-a-problem.md). Never include a participant's details.
