# Support more than one person with one sign-in

If you support more than one participant, you can now see each person's records with **one sign-in** and switch between them at any time. Each person's records stay completely separate.

## What's new

- **One sign-in for everyone you support.** Family members, support workers and coordinators who help more than one participant no longer need a separate account for each. The administrator of each person's records invites the email address you already sign in with.
- **Choose who you are supporting today.** After you sign in, the portal asks whose records you want to work in. Nothing from anyone's records is shown until you choose.
- **Switch at any time.** Use **Supporting** at the top of the screen. What you can see and do follows your role *for that person*, so you might be an Admin for one participant and a User for another.
- **Skip the question.** In **Profile → Preferences**, choose whose records open when you sign in, on every device.
- **One person per tab.** Each browser tab remembers whose records it is in, so you can keep two people's records open side by side.

If you support only one person, nothing changes: you go straight in, as before.

## Also in this release

- **Signing out reaches every tab.** When you choose **Sign out**, you are signed out in every tab and window of that browser, and the session cannot be reused. See [Sign in](../../account/sign-in.md).
