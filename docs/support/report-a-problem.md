# Report a problem

Found a bug, or something not working the way you expect? Tell us in our community Discussions on GitHub — you do not need to be technical, and you do not need a Carers Portal account, just a free GitHub one.

[:material-bug: Report a problem](https://github.com/thegippygeek/carersportal-docs/discussions/new?category=q-a){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps enormously. If it turns out to be a bug, we will open an issue to track the fix and link it from your post.

!!! danger "Never include a participant's details"
    Discussions are public. Describe the problem without names, dates of birth, NDIS numbers or anything from a care record. If you must share something sensitive, email [privacy@carersportal.me](mailto:privacy@carersportal.me) instead.

Not sure whether it is a bug or a question? Post it anyway — [Discussions](https://github.com/thegippygeek/carersportal-docs/discussions) is also where "how do I…" questions turn into new pages here.
