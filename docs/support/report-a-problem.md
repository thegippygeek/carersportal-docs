# Report a problem

Found a bug? Tell us on GitHub — you do not need to be technical, and you do not need a Carers Portal account, just a free GitHub one.

[:material-bug: Report a bug](https://github.com/thegippygeek/carersportal-docs/issues/new?template=bug.yml){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

The form asks what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps enormously.

!!! danger "Never include a participant's details"
    Reports are public. Describe the problem without names, dates of birth, NDIS numbers or anything from a care record. If you must share something sensitive, email [privacy@carersportal.me](mailto:privacy@carersportal.me) instead.

Not sure whether it is a bug or a question? Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/thegippygeek/carersportal-docs/discussions) — that is also where "how do I…" questions turn into new pages here.
