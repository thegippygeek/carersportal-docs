# Log fluids, meals and bowel health

These day-to-day records live alongside the shift note for the shift they happened in.

## Fluids
Choose **Fluids**, enter the amount in millilitres and the time. The daily total against the participant's target shows at the top of the page, so you can see at a glance whether they are on track.

## Meals
Choose **Meals**, pick the meal, and note what was eaten and roughly how much. Use the notes field for anything unusual (refused, needed help, allergy concern).

## Bowel health
Choose **Bowel diary** and select the type from the chart pictures; add the time and any notes. This is sensitive health information — it is only visible to people with a role on the participant's care team.

!!! tip
    If you are recording several things from one shift, start from the shift note and add them there — they are linked automatically.
