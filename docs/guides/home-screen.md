# Add Carers Portal to your home screen

There is no app to download from an app store — Carers Portal runs in your phone's web browser. You can still put an icon on your home screen so it opens with one tap, just like an app.

=== "iPhone and iPad"

    You need to use **Safari** for this.

    1. Open Safari and go to [carersportal.me](https://carersportal.me).
    2. Tap the **Share** button — a square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen on iPhone and at the top on iPad. On newer iPhones you may need to tap **•••** first to find it.
    3. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you cannot see it, tap **Edit Actions** or **View More** at the bottom of the list.
    4. Leave the name as *Carers Portal* (or change it) and tap **Add**.

=== "Android"

    These steps are for **Chrome**, the usual browser on Android.

    1. Open Chrome and go to [carersportal.me](https://carersportal.me).
    2. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots) at the top right.
    3. Tap **Add to home screen** (on some phones this is called **Install app**). If it asks, choose **Install**.
    4. Tap **Install** or **Add**, then either drag the icon where you want it or tap **Add** again to place it automatically.

    !!! tip "Using Samsung Internet?"
        Tap the **≡** menu at the bottom, then **Add page to** → **Home screen**.

The Carers Portal icon now sits on your home screen. Tap it to open the portal.

## Do I have to sign in every time?

No. The icon opens the same portal as your browser, so you stay signed in on that phone until you choose **Sign out** or have been away for a long time — see [Staying signed in](../account/sign-in.md#staying-signed-in).

If the phone is shared, always sign out when you finish, even when you opened the portal from the home screen.

## I can't see "Add to Home Screen"

- **iPhone:** make sure you are in Safari. Some other browsers on iPhone do not offer it.
- **Android:** update Chrome from the Play Store and try again.
- Still stuck? You can bookmark [carersportal.me](https://carersportal.me) instead, or [ask a question](https://github.com/thegippygeek/carersportal-docs/discussions).

## Removing the icon

Press and hold the icon, then choose **Remove App** / **Delete Bookmark** (iPhone) or **Remove** (Android). This only removes the shortcut — your account and records are not affected.
