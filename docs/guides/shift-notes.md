# Record a shift note

A shift note is the record of one shift with the participant: who worked, when, and what happened.

1. From the dashboard choose **Shift notes**, then **New shift note**.
2. Pick the **date** and the **shift type** (for example *Day* or *Sleepover*). Start and end times fill in from the shift type; adjust them if the shift ran differently.
3. Write the **notes**. Plain, factual language works best — what you observed, what you did, anything the next person should know.
4. Add any related records from the same screen: fluids, meals, bowel health, medication observations.
5. Choose **Save**.

## Editing or deleting

You can edit or delete **your own** shift notes for **24 hours** after saving them. After that the note is locked to protect the record; ask an administrator if something needs correcting.

!!! note "Only the author can change a note"
    Another support worker cannot edit or delete your note, and you cannot change theirs — even as an administrator, corrections are recorded rather than overwritten.
