# Plans and seats

Carers Portal has two plans. They include the same features, shift notes and care records, and mobile and web access, and differ only in how many people can use your team's portal.

| Plan | Users |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic** | up to 10 users |
| **Standard** | no user limit (unlimited family and workers) |

Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://carersportal.me/#pricing). Every plan bills monthly, quarterly or yearly.

## How users are counted

Each person who can sign in to your team's portal is one **user**, called a seat.

- Every **active member** of your team is one user.
- Every **invite that has not been accepted yet** is also one user, so a pending invite holds a seat until it is accepted or revoked.
- When someone accepts an invite it counts **once**: the invite becomes the member, so they are not counted twice.
- **Participants never count.** Adding or removing a participant does not change your user count.
- **Suspended or removed members** do not count, and neither do **revoked invites**.

On Basic, **Admin → Users** and **Admin → Billing** show where you stand, for example `6 of 10 users`, with the split between members and pending invites.

!!! info "Standard has no limit"
    On Standard nothing is counted against you. You can invite as many family members and workers as you need.
